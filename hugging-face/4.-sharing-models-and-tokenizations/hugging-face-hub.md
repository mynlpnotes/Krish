# Hugging Face Hub

* Central platform that enables anyone to discover, use, and contribute new state-of-the-art models and datasets
* Not limited to Transformers or even NLP. There are models from [Flair](https://github.com/flairNLP/flair) and [AllenNLP](https://github.com/allenai/allennlp) for NLP, [Asteroid](https://github.com/asteroid-team/asteroid) and [pyannote](https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio) for speech, and [timm](https://github.com/rwightman/pytorch-image-models) for vision
* Sharing a model on the Hub automatically deploys a hosted Inference API for that model. Anyone in the community is free to test it out directly on the model’s page, with custom inputs
* Completely Free
* Paid plans to share privately
* [https://huggingface.co/models](https://huggingface.co/models) ⇒ to view the models
