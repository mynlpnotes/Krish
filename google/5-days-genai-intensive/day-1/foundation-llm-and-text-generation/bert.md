# BERT

* Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers
* Encoder only architecture
* Focuses  &#x20;on understanding context deeply by training on a masked language model objective
* Next sentence prediction loss, where it learns to determine  &#x20;whether a given sentence logically follows a preceding one
* Good at tasks that require natural language understanding, such as questionanswering,  \
  sentiment analysis, and natural language inference, among others.&#x20;
* Since this is an  &#x20;encoder-only model, BERT cannot generate text.
