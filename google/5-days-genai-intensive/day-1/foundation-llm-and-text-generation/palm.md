# PaLM

* Pathways language model
* 540 billion parameter
* Developed by Google
* Capable of performing a wide range of tasks, including common sense reasoning,  \
  arithmetic reasoning, joke explanation, code generation, and translation.
* At the time of its release, PaLM was also able to achieve state-of-the-art performance on  \
  many language benchmarks
* Scaled effeciently due to pathway system which google developed to distribute the training of LLM across two TPU v4 pods
