# Gemma

* Family of lightweight, state-of-the-art open  &#x20;models built from the same research and technology used to create the Gemini models
* A key feature of Gemma  &#x20;3 is its multimodality, enabling it to process both text and image inputs and generate text  &#x20;outputs.
* Gemma 3 is available in various sizes, including 1B, 4B, 12B, and 27B  &#x20;parameter models
