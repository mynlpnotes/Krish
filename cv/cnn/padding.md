# 🟢 Padding

* <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**We might miss features at the corners of the image**</mark>
* It can be due to size of kernel, stride
* Types of padding:
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Same: After convolution also the size of the image is same as before**</mark>&#x20;
  * <mark style="color:purple;background-color:purple;">**Valid: No padding**</mark>
  * Zero padding
  * 1 pixel passing
* We can pad with 0 or other colors also
