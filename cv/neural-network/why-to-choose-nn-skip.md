# 🟢 Why to choose NN - Skip

Limitations of traditional methods:

* Algorithm like DT, SVM are good for structured data but not with complex data like images, text or audio
* They rely too much on manual feature extraction

Need for end to end training:

* They enable end to end learning and network learns best features automatically during training

Others:

* Availability of large dataset
* Improvement in hardware, help in training deep NN

Advantages:

* Automatic feature extraction
* Excel in unstructured data
* Non linear activation function, solves complex and non linear relationship
* Generalization -> It will work on unseen data
