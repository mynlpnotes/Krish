# Perceptron Origin

* A hypothetic nervous system or machine designed to illustrate some of fundamental properties of intelligent system
* In 1957, frank rosenbolt simulated perceptron on an IBM 704
* His organization of a perceptron is constructed of three kind of cells (A-I, A-II, R)
* Output from retina goes to projection area
* Light from projection area will come on random areas on Association area
*   From Association we get onto R1 and R2
